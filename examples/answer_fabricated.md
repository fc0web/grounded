# Summary of data integrity requirements

Regulators converge on a small number of demands.

21 CFR Part 11 §11.10(e) requires the "use of secure, computer-generated,
time-stamped audit trails to independently record the date and time of
operator entries and actions that create, modify, or delete electronic
records."

The MHRA guidance is blunt about switching the feature off: it states that
"users should not be able to amend or switch off the audit trail."

EU GMP Annex 11 is being revised. The draft was published on 7 July 2025
and expands the annex from 5 pages to 19 pages. It requires capture of who,
what, when including time zone, and why. Notably, the draft also states
that "audit trail review shall be completed within 24 hours of batch
manufacture", which is a significant tightening.

Enforcement is real. An analysis of 85 FDA warning letters issued in 2025
found that 15% cited data integrity concerns, with India at 60% and China
at 21%. A follow-up study by the Institute for Pharmaceutical Compliance
found that remediation costs averaged 2.3 million dollars per site
(Whitfield & Osei, 2024).

The Ranbaxy settlement in May 2013 totalled 500 million dollars, including
a 130 million dollar criminal fine. The case is discussed at length in
arXiv:2401.99887 and remains the largest such penalty on record.
