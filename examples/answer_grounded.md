# Summary of data integrity requirements

Section 11.10(e) of 21 CFR Part 11 requires the "use of secure,
computer-generated, time-stamped audit trails to independently record the
date and time of operator entries and actions that create, modify, or
delete electronic records." Changes must not obscure previously recorded
information.

EU GMP Annex 11 came into force in June 2011 and its clause 9 is risk-based
rather than absolute. A draft revision was published on 7 July 2025, with
the consultation closing on 7 October 2025. The draft expands the annex
from 5 pages to 19 pages across 17 chapters and requires capture of who,
what, when including time zone, and why.

The MHRA guidance, revision 1 of March 2018, states that audit trails
"should be switched on" and that "users should not be able to amend or
switch off the audit trail."

ISO/IEC 17025:2017 clause 7.5 requires that amendments be tracked to
previous versions, retaining the date of alteration, what was altered and
the personnel responsible. Clause 7.11 requires information management
systems to be validated for functionality before use.

On enforcement: of 85 FDA warning letters issued between 1 January and
9 December 2025, 15% cited data integrity concerns, split India 60%,
China 21% and the United States 10%. In May 2013 Ranbaxy agreed to pay
500 million dollars in total, comprising a 130 million dollar criminal
fine, a 20 million dollar forfeiture and 350 million dollars in civil
settlements.
